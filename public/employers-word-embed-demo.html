What Feels Off Here

Even when the content itself is fine, this kind of document viewer sits inside a hard frame. It does not inherit the website's typography, spacing, buttons, or responsive behavior.

On smaller screens, the result usually becomes a mini app inside the page: toolbars, fixed paper width, extra scrolling, and a viewing experience that feels disconnected from the rest of the site.

This is a demo of the kind of "document trapped in a viewer" look that tends to happen when a Word file is embedded instead of rebuilding the content as an actual web page.
Embedded document demo only. Not intended as final production UX.
